# Baseline 0.3: current roster membership, historical archive, and sample sorting

The dynasty catalog is now an explicit current NFL roster pool. Sleeper's `active` flag is not sufficient: retired players such as Tom Brady still have it set. The pipeline uses nflverse season rosters, matches explicit Sleeper/GSIS IDs, and accepts current ACT/INA/DEV/RES/PUP/RSN/SUS/EXE/E14 records. Each team's latest roster week is used so bye-week teams are not removed. Older reserve records need a matching Sleeper team and IR/PUP status. Retired, released, stale, and unrostered records are excluded, including players with recent historical production. Excluded entries are retained in a review report, not labeled automatically as retired. An incomplete 32-team roster source stops publication.

The shared ranking engine additionally rejects ineligible, retired-status and free-agent records before calculating replacements or rankings. Every UI sorting option uses the same current pool. Zero-value ties use projected production, not historical identity order. Rookie, injured, and practice-squad players on verified current rosters remain eligible even with no NFL statistics.

The Historical archive searches all 1999–present statistical careers, separately from the dynasty catalog. Career season totals and weekly scoring are available, with links to Compare seasons. Retired players have historical profiles without current valuations. The historical index is checksummed and published with each snapshot to R2. Historical outcomes remain valid inputs to training and draft priors; historical-only players cannot become current replacement or trade assets.

Spread uses this season's statistical appearances only. Median and sample SD remain raw observed descriptions, not adjusted forecasts. Median/SD/form sorts first show players with at least four current-season observations; QB samples additionally require a dynasty starter/incumbent role weight of at least .75. Unqualified samples follow in dynasty-value order and are explicitly labeled. One observation has no SD, not zero SD. The historical archive retains old-season scores; charts never fill a missing current season with old games.

QB current availability and dynasty role are separate. An injured same-team quarterback with at least eight prior-season or two current-season games of 15+ passing attempts is treated as an injured incumbent; the strongest qualifying incumbent per team retains dynasty role 1. A temporary depth-chart starter on that team keeps current role 1 but dynasty role .2. Normal backups retain depth-based weights. An Out/Doubtful/IR/PUP quarterback has current-role weight zero. These are disclosed heuristics, not injury-duration forecasts or a prediction of next week's lineup. No player names, return dates, or individual rank overrides are encoded.

This fixes Caleb Williams being treated as a permanent third-string QB because of injury. Case Keenum's one-game median is still visible but cannot lead the qualified scoring-spread list. Geno Smith remains a current player even if his 1QB value above replacement is zero. Model 0.3 is experimental; this release corrects pool membership and role/sample handling, not a claim of improved held-out forecasting accuracy.
